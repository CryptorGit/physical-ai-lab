# exp_013 English abstract

This study investigated whether a single memoryless policy for a simulated Unitree G1 could extend a prior forward WALK/RUN controller into body-frame omnidirectional locomotion while preserving yaw control and eventually incorporating practical stop and restart behavior. Experiments were conducted in Isaac Lab using a command interface composed of vx, vy, yaw-rate, and gait inputs. The final omnidirectional artifact is W1B-R2 iteration 200 (SHA-256 `61dd37f9f1fb7036357d54975c1120c66c30650ed7d5f2e1d4de91174fdca27d`).

The final actor accepts continuous vx/vy commands and formally passed zero-yaw translation at 0.3 m/s in all 16 evaluated directions, spaced at 22.5-degree intervals. It also supports pure yaw and yaw-conditioned moving turns. Diagnosis showed that weak positive-yaw response was a global command-gain bias rather than missing physical capability. A monotonic calibration therefore leaves non-positive physical yaw unchanged and maps positive physical yaw to 1.5 times the actor input. A corrected final-hold endpoint evaluator separated endpoint capability from slower acquisition behavior.

The study then attempted to integrate stopping, stop maintenance, omnidirectional restart, moving-yaw retention, and stop recovery. A separate exp_012 Stage 2Q policy demonstrated practical stopping, including a 24/24 moving-to-stop positive control, but strict static standing remained unresolved. Static label conflicts, teacher-visited versus candidate-visited trajectory differences, deterministic replay, accepted-environment masked PPO, rear-yaw teachers, a multi-checkpoint offline oracle, command/contact history, and a short GRU were investigated. None produced one checkpoint satisfying the complete joint contract; capability interference remained across conditions.

The final conclusion is therefore positive for single-checkpoint omnidirectional locomotion and yaw control, but negative for complete single-actor stop/restart integration under the tested observations, history, teacher trajectories, and PPO conditions. All claims are simulation-only and do not establish Unitree G1 hardware performance.
